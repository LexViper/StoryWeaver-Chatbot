Dorothy chose to follow the golden road to the old temple in the land of Oz. The temple stood ancient and mysterious, its walls glowing with magic... As she entered, the scene unfolded with a joyful and bright atmosphere, revealing...

Afterward, she smiled at the girl who was standing next to her, and took the opportunity to take her into the temple. Afterward when she saw the young man who had passed away, her eyes widened, as she asked who she was.
During her journey, however, this young girl, who happened to be known as the "Xian" or "Yuan" was unable to explain the matter. Her mind began to wander over this mysterious creature and all the other things. In contrast, when the light suddenly became dim and the world became dark, it was no longer able to understand what was happening. When the lights completely stopped, everything around her instantly disappeared and she looked at her face. She was not alone. This dark world would become a part of the human world. There was a lot to learn about the people of this world, but it could not be done in a positive way.
